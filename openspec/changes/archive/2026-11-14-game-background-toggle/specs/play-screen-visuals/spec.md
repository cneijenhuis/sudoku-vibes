## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Header controls
The header SHALL show pause (left), the timer pill centred and the background button (right) in one row. Pause SHALL be a 52x52px glass button with radius 18px, no border, a 0 3px 0 strong-line drop edge, a 24px ink stroke icon and the accessible name "Pause". The timer pill SHALL be a glass pill (radius 20px) with the time in 34px Fredoka 600 and below it, 6px lower, the status line in 15px 500 in the status colour.

#### Scenario: Header contents
- **WHEN** the play screen is shown
- **THEN** pause, the timer pill and the background button appear in one row with pause on the left, the pill centred and the background button on the right, with the specified sizes and accessible names
